About

Personnel, matched to the work.

People supplied for the service. Plant on a separate dry hire.

Padfield supplies people for civil and road packages in Queensland and New South Wales. The service is named first. The person supplied is the one suited to that service: a project manager, an engineer, or someone for pre-contracts and tendering.

Plant does not come with an operator. A 1.7 tonne excavator and a skid steer are dry hire, and they are scoped separately from the person. Nothing starts until the engagement is agreed.
