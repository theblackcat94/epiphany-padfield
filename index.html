About

Civil and road work since 2015.

Epiphany Padfield is a Registered Professional Engineer of Queensland, number 38942, and a Director and Senior Engineer. She completed a Bachelor of Engineering in Civil and Geotechnical Engineering at the University of Queensland in 2016. Since 2015 the work has been civil and road construction in Queensland and New South Wales: live corridors, pavement, bridges, and the engineering that sits with project management, site engineering and tendering.
