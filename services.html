Services

Personnel for the work. Plant on dry hire.

The person is matched to the service. The machine is a separate hire. Scope is agreed before it starts.

Professional

  • Project management A project manager supplied to run the package: program, interfaces, and how the work gets built. Sequencing and specification compliance sit with the engineer supplied to the job.
  • Engineering An engineer supplied for the site, from mobilisation to completion. Drawings, methodologies, unsuitable material, temporary works, and whether the ground matches the design.
  • Pre-contracts / tendering A pre-contracts person supplied for methods, pricing and bid management before award. Constructability, staging and a price that matches how the job will actually be built.

Equipment hire

  • 1.7T excavator Dry hire. The machine only, without an operator.
  • Skid steer Dry hire. The machine only, without an operator.
