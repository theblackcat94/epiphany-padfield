Services

Eight services, scoped per job.

An engagement is one of these, or a defined mix, agreed before it starts.

01

Road project management

Delivery of road upgrades, pavement, intersections and overtaking lanes inside an operating corridor. Sequencing, traffic interfaces and TMR specification compliance sit with the engineering, not beside it.

02

Civil construction engineering

Site engineering from mobilisation to completion. Drawings, methodologies, unsuitable material, temporary works, RFIs and the call on whether the ground matches the design.

03

Bridge works

Design-and-construct support on bridge interfaces, transitions, precast and insitu concrete. NSW Grey Card for bridge concreting. Structural changes stay with the designer.

04

Marine and waterway civils

Boat ramps, dam outlets, cross drainage and scour protection. Constrained access, dewatering and a short window to get the permanent work in.

04
05

Pre-contracts

Methods, pricing and bid management before award. Constructability, staging and a price that matches how the job will actually be built.

05
06

Quality and auditing

Quality management, contract quality representative roles, and lead auditing of management systems: ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and ISO 45001. Hold points against the specification, not a folder of paperwork.

06
07

Project controls

Program, cost, forecasting, subcontract procurement, variations and extensions of time. Productivity against a target, reported before the month closes.

07
08

Project delivery

Embedded with a civil contractor to run packages: safety upgrade works, bridge packages, marine civils, and the day-to-day of getting subcontractors, plant and the client aligned.
